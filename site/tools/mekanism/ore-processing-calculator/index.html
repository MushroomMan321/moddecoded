        What the code actually says

        Raw ore isn't 5x

        Every recipe that takes an ore block has a raw ore version that gives less. An ore block makes 2, 3, 4 or 5 ingots; raw ore makes 1⅓, 2, 2⅔ or 3⅓. Fortune III averages 2.2 raw ore per block, so mining with Fortune still wins: 7⅓ ingots per block at "5x".

        dissolution:  1 ore   → 1,000 mB slurry → 5 crystals
              3 raw   → 2,000 mB slurry → 10 crystals
              1 block → 6,000 mB slurry → 30 crystals
        data/mekanism/recipe/processing/*/slurry/dirty

        ATM10 rewrites four recipes

        ATM10, ATM10: Aeronautics, All the Mons and ATM10 Lite replace four of Mekanism's recipes for every metal they process, in a KubeJS script. An ore block makes 4½, 6 or 9 ingots at 3x, 4x and 5x there, not 3, 4 and 5. In-game, 64 clumps crushed into 128 dirty dust, 64 dirty dust enriched into 48 dust, 64 crystals made 128 shards, and a washer used 20,100 mB of dirty slurry and 100,525 mB of water for 12,057 mB clean.

        washing:   5 dirty + 25 water → 3 clean
injecting: 1 crystal     → 2 shards
crushing:  1 clump       → 2 dirty dust
enriching: 4 dirty dust  → 3 dust
        kubejs/server_scripts/mods/Mekanism/ore_processing.js

        Raw ore blocks are 9 raw ore per operation

        A Block of Raw Iron goes through the first machine in one operation, at the same ratio as 9 raw ore, for 2 mB of gas a tick instead of 1. Crafting raw ore into blocks first means 9× fewer front machines and 4.5× less gas there. In-game, a Block of Raw Iron made 18 clumps for 364 mB of oxygen, 2.0× what one raw iron used.

        purifying:  1 raw   + 1 O₂/t → 2 clumps
            1 block + 2 O₂/t → 18 clumps
        clump/from_raw_block.json · shard/from_raw_block.json

        Speed upgrades: faster, hungrier

        Each speed upgrade divides the time by the upgrade multiplier to the ⅛ power, but power per tick goes up twice as fast. With no energy upgrades, 8 speed upgrades make each item cost 10× the power (16× in ATM10). In-game on ATM10's 16, a Purification Chamber and a Crystallizer with 8 speed and 8 energy upgrades took 12 ticks an item, at exactly 38,400 and 76,800 J.

        ticks  = base × M^(−speed/8)
power  = usage × M^((2·speed − energy)/8)  per tick
M = maxUpgradeMultiplier (10; ATM10 sets 16)
        MekanismUtils.getTicks · getEnergyPerTick

        Gas per item goes up with speed too

        Purification, injection and dissolution draw gas every tick they run. Speed upgrades raise the draw by M^(2·speed/8) while only shortening the operation by M^(speed/8), so each item uses more gas. Eight chemical upgrades cancel 8 speed upgrades exactly.

        gas per tick ≈ amount × M^((2·speed − chemical)/8)
gas per item = that × ticks
        MekanismUtils.getGasPerTickMeanMultiplier

        The gas draw is random

        Each tick the machine draws a Poisson-distributed amount, capped at 3× the mean. With no upgrades that averages about 0.91 mB a tick, not 1: a Purification Chamber uses about 181 mB of oxygen per item. In-game it used 180 mB per raw iron with no upgrades, and 193 mB with 8 speed, energy and chemical upgrades (the formula says 191).

        used = amount × inversePoisson(mean)
cap  = 3 × ceil(mean)
        StatUtils.inversePoisson · usage.randomizedConsumption

        Crystallizers are the slow step

        A Dissolution Chamber finishes an ore in 100 ticks, enough slurry for 5 crystals. A Crystallizer takes 200 ticks per crystal. With the same upgrades you need 10 crystallizers for every dissolution chamber (6 in ATM10, where washing turns 5 mB into 3), and crystallizers have no factory.

        dissolution:  100 ticks → 5 crystals' worth
crystallizer: 200 ticks → 1 crystal (200 mB)
        TileEntityChemicalDissolutionChamber · TileEntityChemicalCrystallizer

        Washers, separators and infusers double

        These run several operations a tick: 2^speed of them. Each operation still pays the speed power formula, so a washer's power per mB of slurry still climbs with every upgrade. Electrolytic Separators are the exception: 400 J per operation whatever you fit. In-game, washers with 4 and 8 speed upgrades washed exactly 16 and 256 operations a tick.

        washer ops/tick = 2^speed   (1 mB slurry + 5 mB water each;
                             ATM10: 5 mB + 25 mB → 3 mB)
separator: 400 J per op, fixed
        TileEntityChemicalWasher · TileEntityElectrolyticSeparator

        Items straight into the gas slot

        The gas slot on these machines converts items. Flint gives 10 mB of oxygen, salt 2 mB of hydrogen chloride, sulfur dust 2 mB of sulfuric acid. Through a Chemical Oxidizer and two infusers, one sulfur dust makes 100 mB of acid instead of 2.

        flint  → 10 O₂     salt → 2 HCl
sulfur → 2 H₂SO₄   (or 100 via the oxidizer)
        recipe/chemical_conversion · ChemicalInventorySlot.fillOrConvert

        Factories cost the same per item

        A factory is several single machines in one block: each slot runs at the machine's speed and draws the machine's power, and the upgrades in the factory apply to every slot. Factories save space and upgrades, not power.

        slots: Basic 3 · Advanced 5 · Elite 7 · Ultimate 9
power: machine usage × busy slots
        TileEntityFactory · FactoryTier · blocktype.Factory
