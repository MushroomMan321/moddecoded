How it goes together

Fusion reactor

  1. It is always 5×5×5 with the corners and edges cut away. On each face, the ring of 8 around the middle plus must be Fusion Reactor Frame. The middle plus of 5 can be frame, Reactor Glass, ports, a logic adapter or the laser focus matrix. The 3×3×3 inside stays air.
  2. The Fusion Reactor Controller goes in the centre of the top face, and nowhere else.
  3. Put a Laser Focus Matrix where your laser can reach it, and at least two Fusion Reactor Ports. Sneak-right-click a port with a Configurator to switch it between input and output.
  4. Pipe deuterium and tritium (or D-T fuel) into an input port, and water too if it's water-cooled. Power and steam leave through an output port.

Lighting it

  1. Fill a Hohlraum with 10 mB of D-T fuel (a Chemical Infuser makes D-T fuel from deuterium and tritium) and put it in the controller.
  2. Set the injection rate on the controller's Fuel tab.
  3. Point a Laser Amplifier at the focus matrix and feed it with Lasers, one per side. Set its minimum threshold to 400M FE so it fires everything in one shot.
  4. When the amplifier fires, the plasma jumps to 100 million K, the hohlraum is used up and the reaction runs on its own. Keep out of the core while it burns.

Industrial turbine

  1. Edges are Turbine Casing. Faces can be casing, Structural Glass, Turbine Valves or Turbine Vents. Width and depth must be odd.
  2. Stack Turbine Rotors up the center column from the floor, then right-click each rotor with Turbine Blades (two per rotor at most).
  3. Put the Rotational Complex on top of the rotors and fill the rest of that layer, wall to wall, with Pressure Dispersers.
  4. The first Electromagnetic Coil goes directly on top of the complex; every other coil must touch a coil. Saturating Condensers go anywhere above the dispersers.
  5. Steam goes in and power comes out through the valves. With condensers, the vents output water you can pipe back to the reactor.

Turbine size

Width and depth must be odd, from 5 to 17. Height 5 to 18.

    What the code actually says

    It lights from one 400M FE shot, not a trickle

    Laser energy heats the cold plasma by energy ÷ 10 K, and burning starts at 100 million K. The plasma also leaks 0.2% of its lead over the case every tick, so a steady beam levels off far below that. A Laser Amplifier has to release 1 GJ (400M FE) at once.

    not burning: T += energy / 100 × 10
burns when:  T ≥ 100,000,000 K
needed ≈ 10 × (1e8 − 300) J = 1 GJ
    FusionReactorMultiblockData.addTemperatureFromEnergyInput

    Water cooling roughly triples the power

    All the fuel's heat leaves through the case. Water takes a k/(k+c) share as steam, worth 20% back through a turbine; the rest goes to the air, and thermocouples keep only 5% of that.

    heat/t  = fuelEnergy × injection
passive = 0.05 × c/(k+c) × heat
steam   = 0.2 × k/(k+c) × heat / 10   mB/t
    FusionReactorMultiblockData.transferHeat

    It can't melt down

    There is no damage counter. Run out of water or back up the steam and that heat goes to the air instead, so the reactor just falls back toward its air-cooled output. The only danger is the core: anything inside the 3×3×3 takes 50,000 damage while it burns.

    water short → caseWaterHeat = 0
kill(): entity.hurt(FUSION, 50_000)
    FusionReactorMultiblockData.tick · kill

    Too low an injection rate and it goes out

    Each tick it burns the plasma's lead over 100 million K, capped by the fuel injected. If the plasma it settles at is under 100 million K plus the injection rate, burning stops. With mod defaults the lowest stable rate is 2.

    burned = min(T − 1e8, fuel)
plasma = case + 5 × heat/t
    burnFuel · getMinInjectionRate

    ATM10: Aeronautics can't keep it lit

    The pack sets fuelEnergy to 100,000, a hundredth of the default. Even at 98 mB/t the plasma settles around 78 million K, under the 100 million it needs, so the reaction dies soon after ignition.

    lowest stable rate: 126 (air), 152 (water)
max injection:      98
    ATM-10-a config/Mekanism/generators.toml

    Water cooling doesn't work at 2 mB/t

    The reactor's water and steam tanks are sized from the injection rate, but only when the rate changes. A reactor starts at 2 and is set back to 2 after every reload, so at 2 they hold nothing and it runs air-cooled. We saw this in-game: water piped in at 2 never entered. Use 4 or more.

    maxWater = rate × waterPerInjection   // only in setInjectionRate,
maxSteam = rate × steamPerInjection   // when the rate changes
    FusionReactorMultiblockData.setInjectionRate · FusionReactorCache.apply

    Full power takes a few minutes

    The case holds almost no heat, but the plasma holds 100 J per K, so the reactor warms with a time constant of 100 × (5 + 1/(k+c)) ticks: 75 s air-cooled and 37.5 s water-cooled with defaults, and about three of those to reach 95%.

    τ = 100 × (5 + 1/(k + c)) ticks
    plasmaHeatCapacity 100 · caseHeatCapacity 1
