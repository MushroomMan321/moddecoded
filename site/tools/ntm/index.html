Which one do you need?

Breeding plutonium or tritium

The graphite pile gives the seconds to plutonium for any layout, and tritium from lithium rods. The research reactor page covers the breeder machine that sits next to a plate-fuel core.

Steam from one block

ZIRNOX is a single block that builds its own shell. The calculator shows how long a rod load lasts and how close the core runs to meltdown at your CO2 setting.

A big, tunable steam plant

The RBMK is the large one: rod type, graphite placement, control rods, boilers and xenon all change the result, and the page replays up to 1,000 seconds of it.

Fusion

The torus calculator checks whether your klystrons clear the recipe's ignition gate, then what the plasma is worth in steam or EU.

One mod, several forks

NTM has no config files for its reactors: the constants live in the code, and the forks change some of them. Each calculator has a Mod preset bar with the original 1.0.27 values and one preset per fork, read straight out of that fork's source.

The differences matter. The RBMK's rod-pull limit is 150 HE in the original and 1,500 HE in every fork we checked, and the Space and Community Edition forks run ZIRNOX steam at 187.5 against the original's 125. Pick your fork before trusting a number.
