Mekanism power in ATM10 vs ATM10: Aeronautics, setting by setting

Two packs with almost the same name tune Mekanism in opposite directions. ATM10 makes passive generators stronger and fission weaker. ATM10: Aeronautics makes fission and fusion much weaker and machines cheaper to run. If you follow a guide written for the other pack, or for default Mekanism, your numbers will be off by up to 125×.

The short version

  • Fission: each mB of fuel is worth ¼ of the default in ATM10 and 1/125 in Aeronautics.
  • Fusion: fuel energy is default in ATM10 and 1/100 in Aeronautics, with the thermocouple share cut from 0.05 to 0.04 (ATM10) and 0.02 (Aeronautics).
  • Solar, wind, heat: ATM10 boosts them 2–4×. Aeronautics leaves most near default, trims wind and bio, and doubles the Nether bonus for heat generators.
  • Turbines: both packs give coils 8 blades each and shrink the steam tank to a tenth, which makes small turbines tank-limited.

Fission: what a mB of fuel is worth

A fission reactor turns every mB of fuel it burns into energyPerFissionFuel of heat. Water carries that heat away as steam at about 50 heat per mB, and a full turbine turns each mB of steam into 10 J (4 FE).

The low heat per fuel has a side effect: an Aeronautics reactor barely warms up, so heat is almost never what stops you. The limit is how many fuel assemblies you build (each allows 1 mB/t of burn) and how much fuel you can supply. The example reactor would stay below the 1,200 K damage point up to about 11,000 mB/t if it had the assemblies for it. Aeronautics also raises the default burn rate a new reactor starts at from 0.1 to 0.15 mB/t.

To put 23,000 FE/t in perspective: in Aeronautics an Advanced Solar Generator peaks at 160 FE/t, so that whole reactor and 17×17×18 turbine equal about 144 advanced solar panels. In ATM10 the same reactor equals about 1,500 of that pack's much stronger panels.

Industrial turbines: the same change in both packs

SettingMod defaultBoth ATM10 packsWhat it does
bladesPerCoil48Half as many coils for the same blades.
ventChemicalFlow32,00043,478Each vent passes 36% more steam.
condenserRate64,000128,000Each condenser returns twice the water.
chemicalPerTank64,0006,400Steam storage per block is a tenth.

That last one matters more than it looks. A turbine can't push more steam per tick than its tank holds, and the tank is rotor height × width × depth × chemicalPerTank. A 7×7×10 turbine with a 6-block rotor shows a max flow of 2.83M mB/t in both packs, but its tank only holds 1.88M, so 1.88M mB/t is its real limit. The in-game "max flow" doesn't show this. Big turbines are fine: a 17×17×18 holds 25.9M against 15M flow.

The config describes fuelEnergy as affecting injection rate, max temperature and ignition temperature, and thermocoupleEfficiency as the fraction of casing heat turned into energy. We haven't traced the fusion reactor's code yet, so we won't put an exact output ratio on it, but in Aeronautics both settings point the same way: a lot less power.

Check your own pack

These all live in config/Mekanism/generators.toml, general.toml and machine-usage.toml in your instance folder. On a server, the server's copy is what counts. Each setting's mod default is written right above it in a # Default: comment, so you can spot a pack's changes by comparing the two.
